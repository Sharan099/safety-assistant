## Answer quality at scale (judged harness, `27f20cf`, 461 questions)

`LLM_MODEL=gpt-oss-20b`, fallback `gpt-oss-120b`, prompt `grounded_v4`, `sac_v2` index, rerank-with-context, reasoning effort low. Deterministic metrics; result files `evals/results/generation_*_20260915T15*.json`. Turns lost to gateway 429 on both models (17 of 461, evidence-only) count against fact coverage and are re-asked on the next run.

Against the ≥ 0.92-per-metric target: refusal accuracy and citation hit meet it on the human and scenario sets; fact coverage (0.87–0.89) and citation precision (0.65–0.80) do not. Precision anatomy on regulatory_v2: of the 113 off-regulation citations, 0 occur when the question names the regulation (retrieval is scoped); 34 are verbatim twin clauses of the expected one (R137 for R94, R135 for R95 — the claim is true, the metric counts the second citation as wrong); the rest are unnamed-regulation questions whose gold picks one of several regulations stating the same text. The metric is left strict; the numbers are reported as they are.
